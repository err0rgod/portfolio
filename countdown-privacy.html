Privacy Policy — Countdown New Tab

Last updated: November 2025

Summary

Countdown New Tab stores all of your settings locally in your browser. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with me or any third party.

What data the extension handles

  • Countdown target date, label, and appearance preferences — stored in chrome.storage.sync, which syncs across your own signed-in Chrome profiles. This data never leaves your Google account's sync storage.
  • Wallpaper image (if you upload one) — stored in chrome.storage.local on your device only.
  • Focus timer state — stored in chrome.storage.local on your device only.

Permissions

  • storage — used to save your countdown settings and preferences.
  • alarms — used to notify you when a focus timer session completes.
  • notifications — used to show the focus-complete notification.
  • theme — used only when you enable "Sync browser theme" to recolor the browser frame to match your wallpaper. Disable it and no theme changes are made.

The extension does not request access to your browsing history, tabs, bookmarks, or any website content.

What the extension does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.
  • No network requests to external servers. All processing happens on your device.
  • No account required. No data is shared with me or third parties.

Data removal

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data. To also clear synced settings, remove the extension and delete its sync data via your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data practices change, this page will be updated before the change ships. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
